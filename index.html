I am an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), working in the Data eXperience Lab (DXLab), advised by Prof. Eunil Park.

I am guided by a simple question: How can AI keep learning from a changing world while remaining something people can trust?

My research focuses on Trustworthy AI, Efficient AI, and Information Retrieval, with the goal of building adaptive and reliable AI systems that can reason effectively and respond to evolving knowledge.

I am particularly interested in enabling large language models (LLMs) to incorporate updated knowledge, perform complex reasoning, and make reliable decisions through effective information retrieval and contextual understanding. I also explore self-supervised, data-efficient, and multimodal learning to develop AI systems that can learn and adapt with limited supervision.

My current research interests include:

  • Self-Evolving AI

    Knowledge Editing, Continual Knowledge Updating, Self-Refinement of LLMs

  • Self-supervised Learning

    Language Model Pre-training, Multimodal Contrastive Learning, Text Embeddings

  • Data-efficient Learning

    In-context & Few-shot Learning, Parameter-efficient Fine-tuning, Synthetic Data Generation

  • Information Retrieval

    Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Multimodal Retrieval, Re-ranking, Positional Bias Mitigation
